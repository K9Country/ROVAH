# ROVAH Brand Reference

## Public brand name

**ROVAH**

## Legal entity

**ROVAH, LLC**

## Primary tagline / brand promise

**Freedom for dogs, connections for people.**

## Usage

Use the tagline on high-visibility brand surfaces where there is enough room for a short supporting line, such as the welcome screen, major marketing artwork, brand/legal introductions, and selected app-store or web metadata. The ROVAH logo may stand alone on compact screens, navigation, and buttons.

## Visual direction

Preserve the existing rustic green-and-gold palette, natural outdoor setting, wood-sign character, and friendly dog imagery through the rebrand unless the owner approves a new direction.
