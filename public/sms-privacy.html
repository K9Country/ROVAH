ROVAH PRIVACY

SMS Privacy Notice

Effective August 4, 2026

ROVAH does not share, sell, rent, or disclose mobile telephone numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text message program

ROVAH sends text messages only to users who separately choose to receive them. Messages may include reservation, account, and message updates. Consent is not required to use ROVAH.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Information we collect and use

For the text-message program, ROVAH may collect a mobile telephone number, your SMS consent choice, consent date and source, opt-out status, and information needed to send reservation, account, or message updates. We use this information solely to operate, support, document, and improve ROVAH communications and services.

Consent and your choices

Users opt in through an unchecked, optional consent control in ROVAH. We record the consent choice and the phone number associated with it. A host will not receive ROVAH reservation texts unless that host has an active SMS opt-in for the same phone number. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP or by turning off text-message updates in ROVAH.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email Privacypolicy@k9country.net. For support, email support@k9country.net.
