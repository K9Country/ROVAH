## Filing checklist before submission

- Confirm that `ROVAH, LLC` is available in Michigan and does not conflict with an existing entity or trademark.
- Confirm that Steven Myers agrees to serve as registered agent and that the registered office address above can receive legal notices during normal business hours.
- Use Michigan's current LLC filing form or online filing system; it controls if it requests fields differently from this draft.
- Keep the state filing confirmation, company operating agreement, EIN confirmation, and banking records together.
- Prepare a separate single-member operating agreement; ownership and management details are normally better documented there than in the public Articles.
